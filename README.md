## `⬡ who i am`

> 5 years. Three continents worth of production systems. One mission.

I started writing software for **BMW's connected vehicle platform** — 50,000+ vehicles, real-time telemetry, no margin for error. Moved to **Infosys** building AI/cloud systems for enterprise clients. Came to the US on an F1 visa, got my **MS in Data Analytics at Clark**, and never stopped shipping.

Now I build **AI agents that orchestrate real work** — multi-agent pipelines, RAG systems, agentic workflows with human-in-the-loop. I also break things professionally: I do **vulnerability research**, and I've found and disclosed real bugs in production SaaS platforms that were leaking user data.

I don't prototype. I ship. And I secure what I ship.
